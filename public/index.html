Offline-first with automatic backup: Attendance is saved immediately on this device, even with no Wi-Fi or internet. When any internet connection returns (including mobile data), the app retries cloud backup automatically. To see records on another browser/device, that device must connect to the internet to download the latest backup. Open this app once while online on each phone, then install it to the home screen. QR scanning itself does not require Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or NFC.
